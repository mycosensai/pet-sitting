What I do

Sitting, walking & spoiling — done properly

Pick what your baby needs. Every service below comes with the same promise: I follow your routine to the letter, I never rush a visit, and you hear from me before you ever have to wonder.

Most loved

Dog walks & drop-in visits

A real walk around your own neighborhood — not a quick lap to the mailbox — plus everything at home handled before I leave.

  • Leash & harness double-checked before the door opens — fit, clips, and ID tags.
  • Water bowl scrubbed & refilled fresh, paws wiped down after the walk.
  • Feeding exactly your way — measured, timed, and noted, treats only if you say so.
  • Play & settle time after — a tired, calm dog, not a wound-up one.
Cats are royalty here

Cat care, the quiet way

Cats don’t want a party — they want their kingdom undisturbed and their person-shaped servant right on schedule. That’s me.

  • Litter scooped every visit, area swept, and I actually check they’re eating and drinking.
  • On their terms — chin scratches for the social ones, respectful distance for the shy ones. Nobody gets grabbed.
  • Windowsill & favorite spots left exactly as found — blankets, toys, sun patches untouched.
  • Proof-of-life photos that actually show their face — no “trust me, they’re fine.”
Travel & holidays

Multi-day & holiday sitting

Headed out for Thanksgiving, a work trip, or a long weekend? Your babies keep their whole routine at home while I keep watch like it’s my own place — because while I’m on duty, it kind of is.

  • Morning & evening visits minimum, more if your pet’s routine calls for it.
  • Home care included — mail brought in, plants watered, lights rotated, trash to the curb on your day.
  • Daily roundup message with photos, appetite, mood, and anything even slightly out of the ordinary.
  • Book holidays early — Thanksgiving and Christmas spots go to returning babies first.
Weekdays, handled

Recurring weekday care

Long workdays, hybrid schedules, the weeks that just won’t quit. A standing visit your dog can set their little internal clock by.

  • Same time, same sitter — consistency is the whole point for anxious and senior pets.
  • Midday walk & potty break, water refresh, and a quick energy burn so evenings are calmer.
  • Puppies & seniors welcome — extra patience, extra potty trips, zero eye-rolling at accidents.
  • Easy to pause or shift — life happens; just text me and we’ll adjust the week.

The precious part

How I take care of what’s precious to you

Anybody can pour kibble in a bowl. That’s not sitting — that’s feeding. Here’s the meticulous part, the stuff I do every single visit whether anyone would ever know or not.

1 before I ever visit

We learn your baby first

  • Free meet & greet at your place, so your pet meets me with you right there — no stranger danger on day one.
  • I write down the whole routine: food brand & amount, meds, vet info, quirks, fears, favorite toy, the spot they always hide in.
  • Keys & codes handled carefully and never labeled with your address. Emergency contact confirmed before you leave.
2 during every visit

Slow, careful, thorough

  • I arrive calm and quiet, let them come to me, and greet them by name — the visit starts on their terms.
  • Food measured, water scrubbed & refilled, litter or yard tidied, meds given exactly as directed and logged.
  • Doors, gates, and latches get the double-check — tug test before I walk away, every time, no exceptions.
  • Real play and real affection — not phone-in-hand sitting. If they want couch cuddles, we couch cuddle.
3 after I leave

You hear everything

  • Photos from the visit plus a short, honest report: ate well, drank, potty, mood, and any little story worth telling.
  • Anything unusual — a skipped meal, a limp, a weird quiet — gets flagged to you right away, not at the end of the trip.
  • Your home left as I found it: lights as you set them, thermostat untouched unless you asked, everything locked and confirmed in my last message.
Minute 0
Arrive & say hello properly

Quiet entry, shoes by the door, a minute to let your pet sniff, decide I’m still their friend, and show me where they’re at today.

Minutes 5–15
Potty, walk, or yard time

Outside first while energy is high. Leash checked, route kept familiar — their favorite trees, their usual corners. Business noted, bags always packed out.

Minutes 15–25
Food, fresh water & the tidy-up

Meal served your way, bowl washed after (not just rinsed), water refreshed, litter or accident spots cleaned properly.

Minutes 25–40
The good part: play & affection

Fetch, feather wand, brush-out, belly rubs, or just sitting together on the porch. This is the part they’ll remember — so it’s never the part I cut short.

Last 5
Settle, secure & send your update

Pet settled in their favorite spot, doors tug-tested, lights set — then your photos and visit note go out before I’m even off your street.

Good questions

Before you ask, cher

Do my pets really stay in my home?+

Yes — that’s the whole idea. I come to them. Their bed, their bowls, their window, their weird favorite corner of the couch. Staying home means no kennel stress, no strange dogs, and no “new place” anxiety on top of missing you.

Can you give medication?+

Yes — pills, drops, and most routine meds, given exactly the way you show me at the meet & greet and logged at every visit, with a photo or note confirming it’s done. If your pet needs injections or anything medical beyond that, tell me upfront and we’ll talk honestly about whether I’m the right fit.

How do keys and lockboxes work?+

However you’re comfortable: hand me a spare at the meet & greet, use a lockbox, or leave one with your leasing office. Spare keys I hold are never labeled with your name or address, and they come straight back to you the day you’re home.

What if my pet is shy, senior, or a handful?+

Those are my favorite kind, honestly. We go slow, the meet & greet does the heavy lifting, and visits run on their comfort — not my schedule. Seniors get extra potty trips and gentler walks; shy babies get patience and space; the handfuls get a good play session and a firm, kind routine.

How much does it cost?+

It depends on how many visits, how many babies, and what their care involves — so I quote you directly and plainly in my first reply, before anything is booked, with no surprise add-ons later. The meet & greet is free, and asking never costs a thing.

Something came up last minute — can you help?+

Text me. Because I only sit close to home, I can often reach your place fast when a flight gets moved, a shift runs long, or life just happens. I can’t promise every time — but I’m the neighbor you can actually ask.
